What they’re made of

Printed in sanded PLA, with large parts and rounded edges. Colors include sand, sage, terracotta, slate, honey, and olive. A requested color depends on the filament on hand.

Not every design will suit every person. If a shape is wrong for someone, the project can talk through a different motion.

Requests

How a request moves

  1. Tell us who it is for.

    A name for the requester, a city, and whether the request is for one person or a care organization.

  2. We read it against what we can make.

    The family looks at the note and at what a small home project can take on.

  3. We follow up about what might work.

    A follow-up is a conversation about a design and a possible handoff. It is not a ship date.

A request does not hold a fidget or set a date.

Who can ask

Families, caregivers, memory-care homes, and adult day programs can all send a request. One person is the usual ask. A care home can ask for a few.

Hendrik prints at home in Bethesda. A handoff is most realistic nearby, in Maryland, Virginia, and Washington, DC.

You do not need to send medical records, a diagnosis, or proof of income.

A project in the family

Hendrik, who is 14, prints the fidgets in Bethesda and gives them away. His sister Charlotte helps with outreach. Adults help oversee the work. The idea sits beside The Lemonade Society, the book club they help lead, which gives books where they are needed instead of selling them.

Questions

Does a fidget cost anything?

No. Fidgets are given away. There is nothing to buy on this site.

Who may request one?

A family member, a caregiver, a memory-care home, or an adult day program. Medical records and proof of income are not part of the request.

Where is this based?

Hendrik makes the fidgets at home in Bethesda, Maryland. A handoff is most realistic in Bethesda and nearby Maryland, Virginia, and Washington, DC.

Can I ask for a design or a color?

Yes. You can name a preference or ask for help choosing. What is possible depends on the designs and filament on hand. Not every design will suit every person.

Is one available now?

This is a small project. Sending a request does not hold a fidget or set a date.

What happens after I submit?

When the form is connected to the project’s inbox, an adult follows up about availability. No response time is promised. Until that inbox is connected, the form says plainly that the request was not sent.

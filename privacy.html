Privacy

What a request includes.

This notice matches the form as it works today. It will change when an inbox is connected.

Right now, the form does not send or store requests

The request page asks for a requester’s name, email, relationship or role, an optional organization, city and state, whether the request is for one person or a care organization, a quantity, a design preference, an optional color, an optional note about hand movements, an optional note about whether someone already has a fidget, and permission to be contacted.

No inbox is connected. Until an adult connects one, the browser does not transmit those answers and this website does not save them. The page says so before and after you try to send.

What the form is not for

Please don’t include a recipient’s full name, diagnosis, date of birth, medical records, or a street address. The form does not ask for them. Hendrik’s home address, school, personal phone number, and personal email are not published here.

When an inbox is connected

The form sends the answers to an address an adult configures. A success message appears only after that address replies with success. The intended reader is an adult helping with the project, who would use the note to follow up about availability.

The provider’s name, where the messages live, who can open them, and how long they are kept are not decided yet. Those details will be written here before the form is turned on. This site does not sell request information.
